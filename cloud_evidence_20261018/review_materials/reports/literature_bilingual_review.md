## Airline Disruption Recovery: From Component Optimization to Integrated Decision Making

**EN:** Airline disruption recovery has developed into a mature research area with extensive models for different operational objects and decision levels.

**中:** 航空公司扰动（disruption）恢复已发展成为一个成熟的研究领域，针对不同的运行对象与决策层级拥有大量模型。

**EN:** Existing studies have investigated aircraft recovery, crew recovery, passenger recovery, and network-level recovery, progressively extending recovery decisions from individual operational adjustments to coordinated decision-making across multiple resources.

**中:** 现有研究已探讨了飞机恢复、机组恢复、旅客恢复以及网络级恢复，逐步将恢复决策从单个运行调整扩展到跨多种资源的协同决策。

### Component-level Recovery Optimization

**EN:** Existing recovery studies have developed specialized optimization models for different operational components.

**中:** 现有的恢复研究已针对不同的运行组件开发了专门的优化模型。

**EN:** Aircraft recovery models focus on restoring flight schedules, maintaining aircraft continuity, and reducing propagation effects after disruptions.

**中:** 飞机恢复模型侧重于恢复航班计划、保持飞机连续性，并降低扰动后的传播效应。

**EN:** For example, \citet{bratu2006flight} incorporated passenger impacts into flight recovery decisions, extending recovery evaluation beyond operational feasibility.

**中:** 例如，\citet{bratu2006flight} 将旅客影响纳入航班恢复决策，将恢复评估从单纯的运行可行性扩展出去。

**EN:** Crew recovery studies further considered personnel availability and regulatory constraints to improve the executability of recovery solutions \citep{petersen2012crew}.

**中:** 机组恢复研究进一步考虑了人员可用性与规章约束，以提升恢复方案的可执行性 \citep{petersen2012crew}。

**EN:** A comprehensive review by \citet{clausen2010disruption} summarized the development of airline disruption management and highlighted the diversity of recovery models for different operational resources.

**中:** \citet{clausen2010disruption} 的一篇综合性综述总结了航空公司扰动管理的发展历程，并强调了针对不同运行资源的恢复模型具有多样性。

**EN:** These studies have substantially improved the quality of decisions within individual recovery components.

**中:** 这些研究显著提升了各个恢复组件内部的决策质量。

**EN:** However, because different recovery functions involve different objectives, constraints, and information requirements, existing models primarily optimize decisions within their corresponding decision contexts.

**中:** 然而，由于不同的恢复功能涉及不同的目标、约束与信息需求，现有模型主要在其相应的决策语境内优化决策。

**EN:** How information generated by one recovery function should be transformed and maintained when entering subsequent decision stages is generally not the primary focus.

**中:** 某一恢复功能所生成的信息在进入后续决策阶段时应如何被转化与保持，通常并非其主要关注点。

### Integrated and System-level Recovery Models

**EN:** With increasing operational complexity, airline recovery research has expanded from individual components to integrated and system-level decision models.

**中:** 随着运行复杂性的增加，航空公司恢复研究已从单个组件扩展到集成化（integrated）与系统级（system-level）的决策模型。

**EN:** Integrated recovery approaches consider interactions among aircraft, crew, passengers, and network operations, aiming to improve recovery performance under coupled operational constraints.

**中:** 集成式恢复方法考虑飞机、机组、旅客与网络运行之间的相互作用，旨在耦合的运行约束下提升恢复性能。

**EN:** For example, \citet{shebalov2012optimization} developed an integrated airline recovery model that jointly considers multiple recovery factors.

**中:** 例如，\citet{shebalov2012optimization} 开发了联合考虑多种恢复因素的集成式航空公司恢复模型。

**EN:** \citet{pillac2013review} reviewed uncertainty modeling in airline operations and emphasized the importance of representing uncertain operational evolution in recovery decisions.

**中:** \citet{pillac2013review} 综述了航空公司运行中的不确定性建模，并强调了在恢复决策中刻画不确定性运行演化的重要性。

**EN:** Recent reviews further indicate that airline disruption management has increasingly incorporated predictive information, data-driven methods, and system-level decision support \citep{hassan2021airline}.

**中:** 近期的综述进一步指出，航空公司扰动管理已越来越多地纳入预测性信息、数据驱动方法以及系统级决策支持 \citep{hassan2021airline}。

**EN:** These developments have substantially expanded the scope of airline recovery optimization.

**中:** 这些进展已显著扩展了航空公司恢复优化的范围。

**EN:** However, a broader optimization scope does not necessarily imply a consistent decision process across different modeling stages.

**中:** 然而，更广的优化范围并不一定意味着在不同建模阶段之间存在一致的决策过程。

**EN:** An integrated recovery model may consider multiple operational entities, while the information used for state construction, consequence evaluation, and action comparison may still be represented differently across stages.

**中:** 一个集成式恢复模型可能考虑多个运行实体，但用于状态构建、后果评估与动作比较的信息在不同阶段仍可能以不同方式表示。

**EN:** Therefore, existing research has largely addressed how to improve decision information and how to optimize recovery solutions at a larger operational scale, while the cross-stage consistency of information transformation remains less explicitly formulated.

**中:** 因此，现有研究在很大程度上解决了如何改进决策信息、以及如何在更大的运行尺度上优化恢复方案，而信息转化的跨阶段一致性仍较少被明确形式化。

## Information Sharing and State-dependent Decision Making

**EN:** Information sharing and state representation provide two complementary foundations for dynamic decision systems.

**中:** 信息共享与状态表示（state representation）为动态决策系统提供了两个互补的基础。

**EN:** In aviation, information-sharing mechanisms improve the availability of operational information among stakeholders.

**中:** 在航空领域，信息共享机制提升了运行信息在各利益相关方（stakeholders）之间的可用性。

**EN:** In broader decision-making methodologies, state representation determines how evolving observations are converted into decision-relevant states.

**中:** 在更广义的决策方法论中，状态表示决定了不断演化的观测如何被转化为与决策相关的状态。

**EN:** However, the connection between information availability and consistent state-dependent decision processes remains insufficiently characterized.

**中:** 然而，信息可用性与一致的状态依赖（state-dependent）决策过程之间的联系仍缺乏充分刻画。

### Operational Information Sharing in Aviation Systems

**EN:** Airline operations involve multiple stakeholders, including airlines, airports, air traffic management organizations, and ground service providers.

**中:** 航空公司运行涉及多个利益相关方，包括航空公司、机场、空中交通管理组织以及地面服务提供商。

**EN:** Information-sharing mechanisms have therefore become an important foundation for coordinated operational decision-making.

**中:** 因此，信息共享机制已成为协同运行决策的重要基础。

**EN:** Airport Collaborative Decision Making (A-CDM), for example, provides a framework for sharing operational milestones, flight status information, and resource updates among stakeholders \citep{eurocontrol2017acdm}.

**中:** 例如，机场协同决策（A-CDM, Airport Collaborative Decision Making）提供了一个在利益相关方之间共享运行里程碑、航班状态信息与资源更新的框架 \citep{eurocontrol2017acdm}。

**EN:** Industrial decision-support systems further integrate heterogeneous operational data to support large-scale recovery planning.

**中:** 工业级决策支持系统进一步整合异构运行数据，以支持大规模恢复规划。

**EN:** These developments demonstrate that aviation systems have achieved substantial progress in operational information availability.

**中:** 这些进展表明，航空系统在运行信息可用性方面已取得实质性进展。

**EN:** However, information sharing mainly addresses whether relevant information can be accessed by different participants.

**中:** 然而，信息共享主要解决的是相关信息能否被不同参与方获取的问题。

**EN:** Recovery decision processes require an additional capability: determining how available information should be transformed and utilized consistently across sequential decision stages.

**中:** 恢复决策过程还需要一项额外的能力：确定可用信息应如何在顺序决策阶段之间被一致地转化与利用。

**EN:** In rolling recovery, different stages may require different information roles.

**中:** 在滚动恢复中，不同阶段可能需要不同的信息角色（information roles）。

**EN:** Historical operational information may be needed to construct the current unresolved operating state, whereas current execution information may directly affect action generation and comparison.

**中:** 构建当前未解决的（unresolved）运行状态可能需要历史运行信息，而当前执行信息可能直接影响动作的生成与比较。

**EN:** Therefore, information availability does not automatically guarantee consistency of information utilization throughout the recovery process.

**中:** 因此，信息可用性并不自动保证整个恢复过程中信息利用的一致性。

### State Representation in Dynamic Decision Systems

**EN:** State representation is a fundamental concept in dynamic decision-making methodologies.

**中:** 状态表示是动态决策方法论中的一个基本概念。

**EN:** Dynamic programming establishes the importance of state variables that summarize relevant information for sequential decisions \citep{bellman1957dynamic}.

**中:** 动态规划确立了状态变量的重要性，这类变量将序贯（sequential）决策所需的相关信息加以概括 \citep{bellman1957dynamic}。

**EN:** In partially observable environments, POMDP frameworks further emphasize the construction of decision-relevant states from historical observations when the underlying system state cannot be directly observed \citep{kaelbling1998planning}.

**中:** 在部分可观测（partially observable）的环境中，当底层系统状态无法直接观测时，POMDP（部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程）框架进一步强调从历史观测中构建与决策相关的状态 \citep{kaelbling1998planning}。

**EN:** Recent data-driven decision methods, including reinforcement learning, also rely on the relationship between states and actions to derive effective decision policies \citep{sutton2018reinforcement}.

**中:** 包括强化学习在内的最近的数据驱动决策方法，也依赖状态与动作之间的关系来推导有效的决策策略 \citep{sutton2018reinforcement}。

**EN:** These approaches generally assume that an appropriate state representation is available.

**中:** 这些方法通常假设存在恰当的状态表示。

**EN:** This assumption provides an important perspective for airline recovery.

**中:** 这一假设为航空公司恢复提供了一个重要视角。

**EN:** The operating state in disruption recovery is not directly observable but constructed from evolving operational information, including disruption history, aircraft continuity, turnaround conditions, and downstream execution opportunities.

**中:** 扰动恢复中的运行状态并非可直接观测，而是从不断演化的运行信息中构建出来的，这些信息包括扰动历史、飞机连续性、过站（周转）条件以及下游执行机会。

**EN:** Therefore, the challenge is not only how to make decisions based on a state, but also how evolving operational information is transformed into a state and how the resulting state dependencies are preserved throughout subsequent recovery stages.

**中:** 因此，挑战不仅在于如何基于状态做出决策，还在于不断演化的运行信息如何被转化为状态，以及由此产生的状态依赖关系如何在后续恢复阶段中得到保持。

## Decision-relevant Representations for Recovery Modeling

**EN:** Complex recovery decisions require appropriate representations of operational uncertainty, recovery consequences, and available actions.

**中:** 复杂的恢复决策需要对运行不确定性、恢复后果以及可用动作进行适当的表示。

**EN:** Existing studies have developed various representation methods to improve uncertainty description, impact evaluation, and decision comparison.

**中:** 现有研究已开发了多种表示方法，以改进不确定性描述、影响评估与决策比较。

**EN:** However, richer representations do not necessarily lead to better decisions.

**中:** 然而，更丰富的表示并不一定带来更好的决策。

**EN:** Excessive simplification may omit important operational dependencies, while excessive information may introduce redundancy and decision noise.

**中:** 过度简化可能遗漏重要的运行依赖关系，而过多信息可能引入冗余与决策噪声（decision noise）。

**EN:** Therefore, the key issue is not only how to increase information representation capability, but also how to retain decision-relevant information and organize it consistently across recovery stages.

**中:** 因此，关键问题不仅在于如何提升信息表示能力，还在于如何保留决策相关信息，并在各恢复阶段之间一致地组织这些信息。

### Probabilistic Representation of Operational Uncertainty

**EN:** Airline operations involve substantial uncertainty arising from disruption duration, delay propagation, and resource availability.

**中:** 航空公司运行涉及大量的不确定性，这些不确定性源于扰动持续时间、延误传播以及资源可用性。

**EN:** Stochastic optimization, probabilistic forecasting, and scenario-based methods have therefore been widely applied to represent possible future operational evolutions.

**中:** 因此，随机优化、概率预测以及基于情景（scenario-based）的方法已被广泛应用，以表示未来可能的运行演化。

**EN:** Compared with deterministic estimates, probabilistic representations preserve multiple possible operating outcomes and support risk-aware recovery decisions.

**中:** 与确定性估计相比，概率表示保留了多种可能的运行结果，并支持具备风险意识（risk-aware）的恢复决策。

**EN:** Scenario-based recovery models represent uncertain disruption evolution through probability distributions or scenario trees, allowing decisions to account for future variability \citep{pillac2013review,zhaoTwoStageAircraftRecovery2023}.

**中:** 基于情景的恢复模型通过概率分布或情景树来表示不确定的扰动演化，使决策能够考虑未来的可变性 \citep{pillac2013review,zhaoTwoStageAircraftRecovery2023}。

**EN:** However, probabilistic representation also introduces an information selection problem.

**中:** 然而，概率表示也带来了一个信息选择问题。

**EN:** A narrow representation may omit critical operational changes, whereas an excessively broad representation may contain redundant information that increases decision complexity and weakens the identification of relevant dependencies.

**中:** 过窄的表示可能遗漏关键的运行变化，而过宽的表示可能包含冗余信息，从而增加决策复杂性并削弱对相关依赖关系的识别。

**EN:** Therefore, effective probabilistic representation requires not only uncertainty preservation but also appropriate retention of decision-relevant operational structures.

**中:** 因此，有效的概率表示不仅要求保留不确定性，还要求恰当地保留决策相关的运行结构。

### Consequence Representation for Recovery Evaluation

**EN:** Recovery actions may generate multiple consequences, including delay propagation, aircraft continuity changes, passenger impacts, and resource consumption.

**中:** 恢复动作可能产生多种后果，包括延误传播、飞机连续性变化、旅客影响以及资源消耗。

**EN:** Existing studies have therefore extended recovery evaluation beyond simple delay measures toward multidimensional consequence representations.

**中:** 因此，现有研究已将恢复评估从简单的延误度量扩展到多维度的后果表示。

**EN:** Such representations recognize that delay duration alone cannot fully characterize recovery outcomes.

**中:** 这类表示认识到，仅凭延误时长无法完整刻画恢复结果。

**EN:** Different actions may produce different combinations of operational and service impacts even when they result in similar delay reductions.

**中:** 即使不同动作带来相似的延误减少，它们也可能产生不同的运行影响与服务影响组合。

**EN:** However, multidimensional consequence representation also introduces consistency requirements.

**中:** 然而，多维度的后果表示也引入了致性要求（consistency requirements）。

**EN:** Different recovery stages may adopt different consequence scopes, evaluation metrics, or impact horizons.

**中:** 不同的恢复阶段可能采用不同的后果范围、评估指标或影响视野（impact horizons）。

**EN:** If consequence representations are not aligned, action comparisons may reflect differences in evaluation definitions rather than differences among recovery alternatives.

**中:** 如果后果表示未能对齐（aligned），动作比较所反映的可能是评估定义之间的差异，而非恢复方案之间的差异。

### Action Representation and Recovery Comparison

**EN:** Airline recovery research has developed various action generation and comparison approaches, including flight adjustment, aircraft reassignment, cancellation, and passenger recovery actions.

**中:** 航空公司恢复研究已开发了多种动作生成与比较方法，包括航班调整、飞机重分配、取消航班以及旅客恢复动作。

**EN:** These approaches construct feasible action sets based on operational constraints and evaluate alternatives using cost, risk, or service-related objectives.

**中:** 这些方法基于运行约束构建可行动作集合，并使用成本、风险或服务相关目标来评估各备选方案。

**EN:** With increasing decision complexity, action spaces have become more diverse, and data-driven methods have also been explored for learning recovery strategies.

**中:** 随着决策复杂性的增加，动作空间（action spaces）变得更加多样，数据驱动方法也被探索用于学习恢复策略。

**EN:** However, a larger action space does not necessarily guarantee better decisions.

**中:** 然而，更大的动作空间并不一定保证更好的决策。

**EN:** If candidate actions are evaluated using different information bases, state representations, or consequence scopes, the resulting ranking may reflect evaluation inconsistency rather than actual action quality.

**中:** 如果候选动作使用不同的信息基础（information bases）、状态表示或后果范围进行评估，所得排名反映的可能是评估的不一致性，而非动作的实际质量。

**EN:** Therefore, effective recovery comparison requires not only feasible action generation but also consistency among state representation, consequence evaluation, and action assessment.

**中:** 因此，有效的恢复比较不仅要求可行的动作生成，还要求状态表示、后果评估与动作评估之间保持一致。

## Research Gap: From Integrated Recovery to Decision-chain Consistency

**EN:** Existing airline recovery studies have established important capabilities in information sharing, predictive modeling, integrated optimization, and state-dependent decision-making.

**中:** 现有的航空公司恢复研究已在信息共享、预测建模、集成优化以及状态依赖决策方面建立了重要能力。

**EN:** These developments have substantially improved recovery decisions by providing richer operational information and broader optimization frameworks.

**中:** 这些进展通过提供更丰富的运行信息与更广的优化框架，显著改进了恢复决策。

**EN:** However, these capabilities have generally evolved around individual decision functions.

**中:** 然而，这些能力通常是围绕各个独立的决策功能演进的。

**EN:** Prediction models focus on obtaining more accurate operational information, integrated optimization models focus on coordinating more operational objects, and dynamic decision models focus on selecting actions based on states.

**中:** 预测模型侧重于获取更精确的运行信息，集成优化模型侧重于协调更多的运行对象，而动态决策模型侧重于基于状态选择动作。

**EN:** How these capabilities should be connected into a consistent recovery decision chain remains insufficiently formalized.

**中:** 这些能力应如何在一条一致的恢复决策链中连接起来，仍缺乏充分的形式化。

**EN:** Specifically, three limitations remain.

**中:** 具体而言，仍存在三个局限。

**EN:** First, higher information quality does not necessarily ensure effective downstream utilization, because information may change its role when transferred across decision stages.

**中:** 第一，更高的信息质量并不一定能确保有效的下游利用，因为信息在跨决策阶段传递时可能改变其角色。

**EN:** Second, broader optimization scope does not necessarily guarantee global decision consistency, because different stages may represent the same operational realization differently.

**中:** 第二，更广的优化范围并不一定能保证全局决策一致性，因为不同阶段可能以不同方式表示同一运行实现（operational realization）。

**EN:** Third, although state-dependent decision methods emphasize the importance of state representations, the process by which evolving operational information forms and maintains such states remains insufficiently addressed.

**中:** 第三，尽管状态依赖决策方法强调了状态表示的重要性，但不断演化的运行信息形成并维持此类状态的过程仍未被充分处理（addressed）。

**EN:** Therefore, this study focuses on establishing a cross-stage decision chain that connects operational information, recovery states, consequence representations, and action comparisons within a consistent decision process.

**中:** 因此，本研究致力于建立一条跨阶段的决策链，在一致的决策过程中连接运行信息、恢复状态、后果表示与动作比较。

### 译者注（本节新增术语，供你与全文统一译法）

- **state representation**：状态表示；**state-dependent**：状态依赖（的）。
- **A-CDM (Airport Collaborative Decision Making)**：机场协同决策，航空业标准术语。
- **POMDP**：部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（Partially Observable Markov Decision Process）。
- **stakeholders**：利益相关方（航空公司、机场、空管、地面服务等）。
- **information roles**：信息角色——指同一信息在不同恢复阶段承担的不同功能（如构建状态 vs. 直接比较动作）。
- **scenario-based / scenario trees**：基于情景的 / 情景树。
- **risk-aware**：具备风险意识的（恢复决策）。
- **impact horizons**：影响视野/影响时域——评估后果所覆盖的时间范围。
- **operational realization**：运行实现（沿用 Introduction 译法，指某一具体运行状态的实际落地）。
